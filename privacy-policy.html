Privacy Policy

The Sound Archive — Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you authorize The Sound Archive via TikTok, we access your basic profile information (username, display name) and the ability to publish videos on your behalf. We do not collect passwords, private messages, or follower lists.

How we use it

We use your TikTok authorization exclusively to publish curated music content videos to your TikTok account. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data storage

Your TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored securely on our server. No personal data is stored beyond what is necessary for the publishing functionality.

Data deletion

You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings. Upon revocation, all stored tokens are automatically invalidated.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us at thesoundarchive.music@gmail.com
